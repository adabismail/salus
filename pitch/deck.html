The problem most businesses never see

Most churn isn't people leaving.
It's payments failing.

Voluntary churn

The customer decided to cancel. A product problem — you can see it, and argue with it.

Involuntary churn

A payment silently failed — expired card, insufficient funds, a bank decline. The customer never chose to leave. They were just dropped.

The numbers
20–40%
of all subscription churn is involuntary — failed payments, not cancellations
4–9%
of monthly recurring revenue lost to failed payments — every month
Why those payments fail
Expired cards25–30%
Insufficient funds20–25%
Bank soft declines15–20%
Sources · Baremetrics · Recurly 2024 SaaS Payment Recovery Report · Churnkey benchmarks
The gap

40–85% is recoverable.
Most of it never gets recovered.

These are soft, fixable failures — a retry, a fresh link, a nudge would win them back. But that means working every failure by hand. So it doesn't happen, and the money quietly walks out the door.
